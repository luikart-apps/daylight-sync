# Daylight Sync

Natural sunrise-to-sunset light for Arcadia LumenIZE lamps, computed from your location or from
your plant's or animal's native habitat. Android app; nothing leaves the phone.

* **Privacy policy and contact:** https://luikart-apps.github.io/daylight-sync/
* **Suggestions and problems:** open an issue on this repository.
