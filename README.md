# Daylight Sync

Natural sunrise-to-sunset light for Arcadia LumenIZE lamps and TP-Link Kasa smart plugs, computed from
your location or from your plant's or animal's native habitat. Android app; it talks only to your own
devices on your own network, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

* **Privacy policy and contact:** https://luikart-apps.github.io/daylight-sync/
* **Suggestions and problems:** open an issue on this repository.
