Daylight Sync

Natural sunrise-to-sunset light for LumenIZE lamps, from your location or your plant's or animal's native habitat.

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Daylight Sync writes a sunrise-to-sunset schedule into Bluetooth lamps (Arcadia LumenIZE range) and, if you add them, into Kasa smart plugs on your home Wi-Fi, once a day. It is built to keep everything on your phone and your own network.

What the app does not do

  • It talks only to your own devices. The app has the Android internet permission for one reason: to reach Kasa smart plugs on your home Wi-Fi (local network only). It never contacts our servers, TP-Link's cloud or anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
  • It does not share data with third parties. There is nothing to share.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Approximate location, to the nearest whole degree (roughly 60 miles / 100 km), used only to work out sunrise and sunset. Finer precision is never stored. On Android 12 and newer the app asks only for approximate location; on Android 11 and older, precise location permission is required by Android for Bluetooth scanning, but the value kept is still rounded to a whole degree. You can also type a location instead of using the phone's.
  • A place you pick on the world map ("native habitat"), if you use that feature. This is a place you choose, not where you are.
  • Your lamps' Bluetooth addresses and names, and the lamp password you enter, so the app can reconnect to the same lamps.
  • Your Kasa plugs' device ids, names and addresses on your Wi-Fi, if you add any, so the app can find the same plugs again.
  • The e-mail of your Kasa account, and its password if you choose to enter it, because newer Kasa plugs only answer the account that set them up, even on your own Wi-Fi. They are kept on the phone, left out of Android backups, used only to talk to your plugs, and never sent to TP-Link, to us, or to anyone else.
  • Your schedule settings and the time of the last sync.

All of this lives in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): to find the lamps and write their schedules.
  • Network (internet permission): to find Kasa plugs on your Wi-Fi and write their daily on/off rules. Only addresses on your local network are ever contacted.
  • Approximate location: optional, only when you tap "Use phone location".
  • Run after restart: to re-arm the once-a-day background sync after the phone reboots.
  • Ignore battery optimisations: requested only if you switch on daily sync, because some phones otherwise stop the background sync after a few days.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
