Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Daylight Sync writes a sunrise-to-sunset schedule into a Bluetooth lamp (Arcadia LumenIZE range) once a day. It is built to keep everything on your phone.

What the app does not do

  • It has no internet access. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere — not to us, not to anyone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
  • It does not share data with third parties. There is nothing to share.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Approximate location, to the nearest whole degree (roughly 60 miles / 100 km), used only to work out sunrise and sunset. Finer precision is never stored. On Android 12 and newer the app asks only for approximate location; on Android 11 and older, precise location permission is required by Android for Bluetooth scanning, but the value kept is still rounded to a whole degree. You can also type a location instead of using the phone's.
  • A place you pick on the world map ("native habitat"), if you use that feature. This is a place you choose, not where you are.
  • Your lamp's Bluetooth address and name, and the lamp password you enter, so the app can reconnect to the same lamp.
  • Your schedule settings and the time of the last sync.

All of this lives in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): to find the lamp and write its schedule.
  • Approximate location: optional, only when you tap "Use phone location".
  • Run after restart: to re-arm the once-a-day background sync after the phone reboots.
  • Ignore battery optimisations: requested only if you switch on daily sync, because some phones otherwise stop the background sync after a few days.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
